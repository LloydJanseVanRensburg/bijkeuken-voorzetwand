Before you close the wall

  • Open the valve and leave the water on for a day. Run a dry tissue along the pipe and the coupling: it must stay dry.
  • Take a photo of the pipe's route. Later, keep skirting nails and screws above 60 mm, or short.
  • Leave the gap behind the frame empty. The glass wool goes in the frame only.
