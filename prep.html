Get ready

Work through the phases in order. Each one opens when the previous one is done, and your ticks stay saved on this phone. A red Stop if not OK means: don't carry on past that point until it's sorted. Message me if one of those fails.

The frame stands 35 mm off the back wall

That way the black water pipe runs free behind it (see Pipes). Every "out from the back wall" number on this page already includes the 35 mm. For how each joint goes together, see Brackets.

Golden rule

Never drill into the back wall. The frame is fixed to the floor, the ceiling joists, the pillar sides and the kitchen wall only.
