Fridge cupboard & laundry corner

A floor-to-ceiling cupboard from the exterior wall to the dryer. The old built-in fridge goes in the right column, next to the dryer. The left column holds brooms, the vacuum and the mop, with top cupboards over both. A worktop runs over the dryer and washer, with wall strips above it for shelves, a hanging rail and baskets.

SKETCH The fridge height (1450) and the gap to the dryer (1310) are guesses from the photo. Measure them first, then the drawings get exact sizes and a cut list.

Measure before the next version

  • Fridge label (inside, on the side wall): brand and model. Also measure its height, width and depth.
  • Exterior wall → dryer side, at floor height and at 1 m.
  • Ceiling height where the cupboard goes, at the wall and 60 cm out.
  • Free space in front: wall face to whatever is opposite (wall, door, frame).
  • Dryer and washer: height, width and depth of each, plus how far the hoses stick out at the back.
  • Washer → end of the wall on the kitchen side, and what's there: a wall you can screw into, or the door frame.
  • Tap, drain and socket in the open bay: height of each, so the worktop leaves room for them.
  • Wall strips: the product name or a link, and the lengths you can buy.
