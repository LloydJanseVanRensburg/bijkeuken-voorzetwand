    Before

    The bare back wall: the brick pillar, the black water pipe on the floor and the washer corner.

  • Stage 13 Nov

    Frame up

    44×94 frame standing 35 mm off the back wall, so the water pipe runs free behind the floor plates. Angle brackets on every joint, small brackets holding V3 and V4 to the pillar front, and the open bay left free for the washer.

  • Stage 24 Nov

    Insulation

    Glass wool clamped into every bay, flush with the stud faces. Thinner wool in front of the pillar, and the open bay stays empty.

  • Stage 34 Nov

    OSB

    18 mm OSB in three rows, every panel labelled A1–D3, with joints on the studs and noggins. The washer bay stays open for the tap, drain and socket.
